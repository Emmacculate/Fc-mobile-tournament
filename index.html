Tournament Rules

  • Format: 10 Players divided into 2 groups (A & B) of 5 players each.
  • Group Matches: 90-minute Head-to-Head (H2H) match. Draws are allowed in the group stage (3 pts for win, 1 pt for draw, 0 for loss).
  • Tie-Breakers: 1. Points → 2. Goal Difference → 3. Goals For → 4. Head-to-head.
  • Knockout Stage: Extra Time & Penalties must be enabled in H2H friendly invites.
  • Disconnections: Replay remaining minutes or submit proof/screenshot to the tournament host.
